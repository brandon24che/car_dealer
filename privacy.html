info

AutoSelect is a demonstration project. Vehicle listings, reviews, pricing and contact details are sample data, and no real vehicles are sold through this site.

We keep this policy short and readable. If anything is unclear, contact us using the details at the end of the page and we will walk you through it.

1. Overview

This policy covers the AutoSelect website, including the inventory catalogue, vehicle detail pages, the car match quiz, saved vehicles, trade-in estimates and the concierge contact form. It does not cover third-party sites we link to, such as WhatsApp or Google Maps, which have their own privacy policies.

By using the site, you agree to the collection and use of information described here. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, email address and phone number when you send a message, book a test drive or request a trade-in estimate.
  • Vehicle interests — saved vehicles, comparison selections, quiz answers, financing preferences and the stock numbers you ask about.
  • Trade-in details — year, make, model, mileage, condition, VIN (if you provide it) and photos you upload.
  • Account information — display name, email and, optionally, phone number if you create an AutoSelect account.
  • Message content — the text of concierge requests and any information you choose to include.

Information collected automatically

  • Browser type, device type, screen size, language, approximate region and referring page.
  • Pages viewed, searches run, filters applied and interactions such as saving or comparing a vehicle.
  • Standard server logs, including IP address, timestamps and error diagnostics, retained by our hosting provider.

We do not ask for and do not want Social Security numbers, bank account details or copies of identity documents through this website.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to your questions and route them to the right specialist.
  • Show relevant inventory, remember saved vehicles and resume your match quiz.
  • Prepare finance estimates, trade-in ranges and test-drive appointments.
  • Operate, secure and improve the site, including diagnosing errors and preventing abuse.
  • Send transactional messages about a request you made.
  • Send marketing messages only where you have opted in, and stop when you unsubscribe.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

Where applicable, we rely on performance of a contract (handling your request), legitimate interests (operating and improving the site) or consent (optional marketing and non-essential cookies).

5. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Service providers — hosting, authentication, database and email providers that process data on our instructions, including Google Firebase when the site is connected to a live project.
  • At your direction — when you continue a conversation on WhatsApp, your message and contact details are handled under WhatsApp's terms.
  • Legal requirements — when required by law, subpoena or to protect the rights, property or safety of AutoSelect, our customers or the public.
  • Business transfer — in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

6. Security

We use HTTPS in transit, role-based access controls and database security rules that restrict reads and writes to the minimum needed. Access to concierge and test-drive records is limited to authorised staff.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If you believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately at privacy@autoselect.demo.

7. Retention

We keep concierge requests, test drives and trade-in valuations for up to 24 months so we can follow up and honour any offer, then delete or aggregate them. Account records are kept while your account is active and for a short period afterwards. Browser storage remains until you clear it or it is overwritten.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Access and correction — request a copy of the information we hold about you or ask us to correct it.
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and associated requests, subject to legal retention duties.
  • Marketing — opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in a message or by contacting us.
  • Local data — clear saved vehicles and preferences directly in your browser.

If you live in California or another region with specific privacy laws, you may have additional rights, including the right to know, delete and opt out of sharing. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. Send requests to privacy@autoselect.demo; we may need to verify your identity before acting.

9. Children

The site is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The effective date at the top of the page will change, and material updates will be highlighted on the site. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
