Frequently asked

Can't find what you need? Message the concierge and we'll answer directly.

How quickly will I hear back?add

WhatsApp is typically answered within a few minutes during hub hours. Emails and form messages are answered the same business day, usually within two hours.

Can I book a test drive before I visit?add

Yes. Open any vehicle and use Schedule test drive, or include the stock number in the form above. We hold the car for your appointment and confirm by text.

Do you buy cars without a purchase?add

Absolutely. Start with the instant trade-in estimate — you can sell outright, and there is no obligation even after an in-person appraisal.

Can you help with financing from another state?add

We arrange financing and shipping across the continental US. Tell us your ZIP code in the message and we'll include transport and tax estimates in your quote.

Where do I send documents or proof of insurance?add

Once your deal is opened, your specialist sends a secure upload link. Please never send Social Security numbers or full bank details by email or WhatsApp.

Is this a real dealership?add

AutoSelect is a demo marketplace built to showcase the vehicle shopping experience. Inventory, pricing, reviews and contact details are sample data.
